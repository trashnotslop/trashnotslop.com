In April of 2012 Pebble Technology, a relatively unknown company, was unable to secure funding from traditional investors, so they turned to Kickstarter. With a goal of $100,000, the project ended up breaking the record for the largest crowdfunding campaign of all time[^1], raising over $10,000,000 from almost 70,000 backers.

One of those backers was me! I don't remember how I learned about the Pebble smartwatch, or why I backed it. At the time I was fairly obsessed with Kickstarter in general, regularly backing projects that I ended up never getting anything from or not really enjoying the end product.

[^1]: A record they would hold until it was broken three years later by their Pebble Time Kickstarter.
