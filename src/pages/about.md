I’m a burned out software engineer with anxiety and ADHD, attempting to have a healthy and productive midlife crisis. The goal of Trash Not Slop is to prove to myself that that I still have worth in this strange new world of generative AI. 

In a time where the level of polish is no longer an indicator of the amount of passion, where everyone is encouraged and empowered to churn out infinite amount of low effort shit, Trash Not Slop is a place where human effort and intention is the top priority.
