To the extent where it is possible in today's modern landscape, all effort has been made to avoid usage of LLMs or another generative AI technology for any and all aspects of Trash Not Slop.

All of the English words and software code[^1] that comprise this website have been written by me, for better or for worse. Everything I create, build, and share will be the work of myself and other humans.

Trash Not Slop will also not be a place for me, or anyone else, to vent, complain, mock, criticize, or in any discuss generative AI, LLMs, or similar technologies.


[^1]: Like all modern software, this web page is built using open source libraries, not all of which are as pro-human coding as I would like.
